HotBot connects an AI trading team to Muse, so using that intelligence can start with an ordinary conversation.

HotBot already had trading agents and paper portfolios. For this hackathon, we brought Muse Spark into the portfolio screen and connected it to our real tools.

Here, I’m asking why it bought a coin. Muse reads the portfolio journal, explains the recorded decisions, and shows the sources behind its answer. I can open the original record, see the exact field, and check when it was read.

Then I say: ask me before the next buy.

Muse turns that preference into a concrete setting proposal: the setting now, what would change, and the tradeoff. It explains that ordinary sells would need approval too, while safety-floor exits continue.

The proposal waits for my review. I get a useful next step and the evidence to judge it.

That’s our vision: HotBot provides the trading intelligence and controls; Muse makes them accessible through conversation. Agentic trading that feels as easy as talking to Muse.

This demonstration uses real paper-portfolio data and Meta responses. Model waits are shortened.
